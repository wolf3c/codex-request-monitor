安装与使用说明：https://github.com/wolf3c/codex-request-monitor#安装与使用

双击「启动监控.command」可打开本机浏览器版；按 Control-C 停止。

传输与请求大小以本机日志为据；网络诊断另核对本机配置和实际连接。WebSocket 大小和下载量缺少记录时显示 -。

English guide: https://github.com/wolf3c/codex-request-monitor/blob/main/README.en.md#install-and-use

Open the dashboard by double-clicking 启动监控.command. Press Control-C to stop.
Use the language selector in the dashboard to switch between English and Simplified Chinese.
Transport and request sizes use local logs. Network diagnostics also checks local configuration and observed connections.
